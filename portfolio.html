AMORMA

A healthcare brand in a crowded market.

We repositioned it around a single idea:
A Mother’s Journey.

From identity to storytelling to AI film —
everything built around one narrative.

Deep Dive: Amorma

The Brief

Mother & Child hospital in Sharjah that provides Ayurvedic postpartum care - in a market where healthcare brands all look and sound the same.

Brand Personality

AMORMA is that kick-ass female friend of mothers. The one who cares, comforts, strengthens, and fights for her friend - against all the odds. Not clinical. Not distant. Present, fierce, and warm.

Storytelling Angle

A mother’s journey. Every touchpoint, every message, every visual - built around a mother's experience from early pregnancy to full recovery.

Brand Identity

Name: 'AMOR' is Love. 'MA' is the universal term for mother. Exactly what the brand stands for.

Logo: Inspired from the Arabic letter 'م' (meem) and the shape denotes a mother, and a child.

AI Filmmaking

We created a fully AI-generated brand film that narrates the Mother's Journey - end to end, which was heavily appreciated by the SHC authorities.

SKILLMOUNT

Lost in a crowded skill academy market.

We repositioned it as:
Your Industry Readiness Partner.

Not a course provider.
A transformation partner.

Deep Dive: Skillmount

The Brief

Skill academies were popping up like crazy. Skillmount, the OG since 2018 was lost in the noise. They needed to stand out, establish a stronger brand presence, and align that presence with their ongoing expansion plan.

Strategy

Reposition the brand from an online skill academy to something more precise and more powerful: Skillmount - Your Industry Readiness Partner. Not a place to learn skills. A partner that makes you industry-ready.

Storytelling

The shock factor : Skillmount - a brand that sells skills saying 'Skills-are-dead' - That's the hook. Answered immediately with 'Industry Readiness-is-alive'. The audience stops. They think. And then it lands.

Media Production

BTB undertook the full media production, featuring brand icon Hanansha. Skillmount reoriented all of its programmes, features, and narratives around the 'Industry Readiness' positioning across every platform and communication.
